# Minimum freeze contract

Record and hash:

- run/asset/venue IDs, code SHA and clean tree;
- family ID, mechanism, anchor, neighbour and causal negative control;
- complete arm configs and unique trial keys;
- provider/schema/data/universe/calendar/corporate-action digests;
- cost, borrow/funding/rollover, fill and terminal-liquidation policies;
- S0 start/end, chronological folds, warmup, purge/embargo and one-bar lag;
- S1 boundary and sealed digest without opening S1 values;
- benchmark, metrics, Stage-A gates, mandatory stresses and verdict policy;
- scored-cell budget, starting observed denominator and published denominator;
- technical-abort rule, publication/idempotency rule and collision handling;
- A7 audit SHA and explicit launch authorization.

State unknown or unavailable fields explicitly. Missing required evidence blocks
launch; it is never filled from a later outcome.
