# Current evidence baseline

**Type:** research  
**Status:** closed

## Question

What does the current repo evidence already establish, so the next research does
not repeat rejected work or treat a per-symbol pass as a portfolio pass?

## Resolution

ETH directional mean-reversion remains the only live research candidate, and its
current evidence is weak enough to require forward testing. The independent
`reclaim_mr`, `trend_pullback`, and `markdown_breakdown` baselines were tested
with funding and shared capital; all three altcoin portfolios failed. A single
symbol passing 2/3 folds is only a screening result, never a promotion result.

See `NOTES.md` sections 14 and 16 and the artifacts under `results/baselines/`.
