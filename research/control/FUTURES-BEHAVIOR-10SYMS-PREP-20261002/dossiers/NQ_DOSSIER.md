## 5. Hypotheses (ranked by testability × causal plausibility — NOT by PnL; all unmeasured; every one carries PREP status: no backtest run)

**H1 — Real-yield shock → NQ underperforms ES (framework §15-1).**
When the 10y TIPS real-yield change over day t exceeds a trailing-quantile threshold (strict past window), NQ should underperform ES over t+1..t+5, because Nasdaq-100 cash flows are longer-duration and more rate-sensitive than S&P 500; strongest in tightening/high-rate-vol regimes, weakest or reversed in easing/AI-capex-boom regimes. Entry: next-session RTH open after signal (entry-delay 1–5 min sensitivity mandatory). Exit: 5-session time exit or revert of yield condition. Direction: short NQ / long ES ratio. Universe/roll: declared calendar rule (note §1 thin-first-2-weeks caveat; alternative volume-triggered roll as robustness). Sizing: volatility-targeted (past-window realized vol); PARAM-GAP: exact threshold quantile + vol-target level must be fixed by pre-registered rule, not tuned. Cost model: commission 0.85 + exchange fee (NOT_FETCHED — use ES-documented 2.24 as placeholder ceiling, declared), spread 6.5 ticks NQ / 1.0 tick ES (S6), slippage NOT_RUN_NO_DECLARED_VALUE. Invalidation: relative-return sign flips in ≥2 individual years or edge dies at 1.5× costs. Counter-hypothesis: yield moves proxy growth-news that lifts NQ (sign ambiguity). Failure: requires revised yield data (use ALFRED vintages). Data: FRED DFII10/DGS10 (free), NQ+ES daily — legal raw true-front window only ~16 months → FLOOR: needs ≥2y vendor history or explicit acceptance of 16-month window; budget: vendor decision is owner's.

**H2 — CoT positioning extremes → conditional NQ vol/asymmetry (framework Positioning bucket).**
When CFTC equity-index CoT leveraged-fund net position (NQ futures-only) is in an extreme trailing percentile (past-window only), NQ forward 1–2-week |return| distribution should widen and skew toward unwind direction, because crowded leveraged length unwinds faster than it accumulates; strongest in stressed-liquidity regimes, weakest in trending melt-ups. Entry/exit: conditional distribution test FIRST (no trade rule until descriptive evidence exists); any later rule = extreme-percentile entry, 5–10 session hold, stop = opposite decile re-cross, time exit 10 sessions. Direction: two-sided conditional. Universe/roll: declared rule. Sizing: risk-parity vs 20-day ATR (past window). Costs: as H1. Invalidation: distributional difference absent year-by-year; or disappears when using release-timestamp (Friday 15:30 ET) rather than position-date Tuesday (PIT discipline test). Counter-hypothesis: extremes persist (momentum), no mean reversion. Data: CFTC CoT historical archive (public/free, PIT release schedule FACT above); NQ daily legal raw. This is the cheapest fully-PIT-testable hypothesis. PARAM-GAP: percentile + holding period pre-registration.

**H3 — Macro surprise × rate response beats macro surprise alone (framework §15-4).**
When CPI/NFP surprise (actual − consensus) is interacted with the same-morning ZN/rate response, the interaction should predict NQ direction over 1d better than the surprise alone, because NQ reacts to the discounting channel, not the news label; strongest when inflation surprises dominate, weakest when growth surprises dominate (sign ambiguity). Entry: 09:00 CT (30 min post-release) — tests whether first-reaction capture is required. Exit: session close / 1d. Direction: conditional on interaction sign. Universe/roll: declared rule (events never in delivery month for the front under this rule). Sizing: unit-risk per event. Costs: spread widening at release NOT measurable (no synchronous quotes — assume 2× median spread, declared as assumption; slippage NOT_RUN). Invalidation: interaction adds no year-stable information; edge dies with 1–5 min entry delay (framework §5-7 requirement). Counter: rate response already prices everything (no residual alpha). Data floor: consensus archive (MISSING — not published by BLS/CFTC; commercial or scraped-with-license source needed; owner decision) + deep intraday for release-window fills. BLOCKED on data.

**H4 — Overnight → RTH continuation conditional on liquidity regime (framework §15-3).**
When the Globex overnight NQ move exceeds a trailing-quantile threshold and the liquidity regime is supportive (e.g., non-stressed funding/normal VIX), the RTH session should continue the overnight direction, because overnight information diffusion is incomplete when participation is thin; weakest in stress regimes (overnight gaps mean-revert). Entry: 08:30 CT; exit: 16:00 CT same session; TP/SL: session-time exit only (event-style description first). Direction: with overnight sign. Costs: spread at open 2× median assumption (declared); slippage NOT_RUN. Invalidation: continuation sign flips across regimes; effect vanishes with 30-min delayed entry. Data floor: M1/H1 with FULL Globex sessions ≥2 years — NOT available on this entitlement (S7 probes n=0 pre-2026; raw hourly is RTH-only, 30D). BLOCKED on data (top acquisition priority for NQ behavior work).

**H5 — Roll-window liquidity/term-structure effects (this dossier's OBSERVED curve).**
Adjacent-quarter premium (~0.8–1.1%/quarter, OBSERVED) should compress/persist predictably around macro-relevant rate repricing because it embeds financing-minus-dividends; and roll-week microstructure (spread widening, volume handoff) should be systematic at quarterly cadence. Descriptive-first: measure spread-change clustering vs FRED rate changes; any rule later. Entry/exit/PARAM-GAP: none specifiable until spread series is computed on ≥2y real-volume data. Invalidation: premium behavior dominated by settlement-quote noise (this snapshot's caveat). Data floor: ≥2y real-volume per-contract NQ + synchronous quotes. BLOCKED on data.
