### 2.1 M1 (RANGEFADE-PIT replication, plan §4.2, §7.2, §8, §9)

### 2.2 M3 (QSTATE-ABSORB, plan appendix A.2)

1. **The spread-WIDE complement control can fail for a COST reason, not an information reason (the single most important finding).** The causal control fades only wide-spread breaks; the executor charges the fill bar's own spread × k (§6).
