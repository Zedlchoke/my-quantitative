## 1. F-PIT-1 — the mode-variable look-ahead: code verdict and adjudication design

### 1.1 Adversarial code verification — SUSPECT CONFIRMED, with two refinements the draft does not have

**(a) The leak is real.** The day-d mode percentile is computed from day d's OWN full-day range and consumed at day start:

- `v2_lib.py:66-79` — `_prev_day_stats` accumulates `hi[d] = max(...)` / `lo[d] = min(...)` over **every** non-skipped bar of day d (traded hours 00..19 UTC). These are full-day statistics; the docstring "PIT helper" is aspirational, not enforced.
- `v2_lib.py:269-271` — `ranges[d] = (hi[d] - lo[d], hi[days[j-1]], lo[days[j-1]])`: element 0 is **day d's own full-day range**; elements 1–2 are day d−1's high/low (the trading levels — these ARE known at day start).
- `v2_lib.py:274-281` — `pct[d] = searchsorted(sorted(hist), ranges[d][0]) / len(hist)`. The trailing window `hist` itself is PIT-ordered (the day's own range is appended *after* its percentile is taken, `:277-279`, capped at 60, `:280-281`). **The only leaking quantity is the query value `ranges[d][0]`.**
- `v2_lib.py:291-301` — at the day-boundary transition (`d != cur_day`, i.e. day d's first bar, 00:00 UTC) the code sets `expand` (mode) from `pct[d]` and the levels from `ranges[d][1..2]`. Day d's full range completes only at the 19:00 bar (~20:00 UTC). **Mode is therefore decided up to ~20 hours before the information exists.** Every in-session bar of day d inherits it (first fillable entry is hour 07, `:302`), so 100% of day-d fills are contaminated. Not a boundary rounding issue — it is a whole-day regime switch selected with same-day future information.

**(b) Refinement 1 — a second, quieter leak: the pct==0 flat-day filter.** `v2_lib.py:293` guards `if d in pct and pct[d] > 0:`. `searchsorted(..., side='left')` returns 0 when the day's own range is ≤ the trailing window minimum, so days whose full-day range ties or undercuts the 60-day minimum are traded NOT AT ALL (`expand=None` → flat day, `:300-301`, `:302-303`). That is a hindsight range filter: the law silently refuses to trade days that *turn out* to be the narrowest. Under the PIT-clean re-specification this exclusion migrates to days *after* a minimum-range day. Any R-ANCHOR implementation that drops this guard is not a one-day shift of the ancestor; the guard must shift with the index.

**(c) Refinement 2 — the draft's own prose mis-states the mode mapping (erratum required before freeze).** With `use_breakout=False`, `:297` gives `expand = wide = (pct[d] > hi_q)`; `:305-310` routes `expand=True` to the **breakout/follow** branch (break above prev-high → long) and `:311-320` routes `expand=False` to the **fade** branch (break above prev-high → short; exit when `abs(c − mid) < (pd_h−pd_l)/4`). ### 1.3 Seam band — critique of the draft and the refined preregistration

Draft §9.4: kill if `|R-ORIG − R-ANCHOR|` net exceeds **median per-session spread cost × 10 round trips**. Critique:

1. **Population-independence defect.** "×10 round trips" is decoupled from the arms' actual trade counts. At ~300 trips the band is negligibly tight (fine); at ~8 trips it exceeds the whole book (kills nothing). A seam that changes 3 trips out of 8 can carry the entire difference and still pass.
2. **Tier ambiguity.** Unstated whether the band unit is the ×1.0 or ×2.0 book. The stress book *widens* the band and weakens the kill; pin to **×1.0 base**.
3. **Magnitude-only test.** A leak that pays a small positive amount on *every* fold is a leak regardless of magnitude; the draft band alone would pass it.

Refined preregistration (frozen now, mechanical at adjudication). Let `S = net_dev(R-ORIG) − net_dev(R-ANCHOR)` at ×1.0 on identical folds; `c_rt` = median full round-trip cost (per-session spread curve, base book); `N_diff` = count of round trips whose (entry_ts, direction) differ between arms (computable from signal arrays pre-PnL). **`KILL_PIT_SEAM` fires if ANY of:**
- (i) `S > 10 × c_rt` (draft band retained as the floor);
- (ii) `S > 3 × c_rt` **and** `S > 0` on ≥ 4/6 dev folds (fold-consistency for small persistent leaks);
- (iii) per-trip mean of R-ORIG on the `N_diff` differing trips exceeds the per-trip mean of R-ANCHOR on matched trips by `> 1 × c_rt` (concentrated-seam probe: the seam buys more than one full round-trip cost per changed trade).

`PASS_PIT` = none fire and M-2 non-vacuity holds. Verdict vocabulary: `PASS_PIT` / `KILL_PIT_SEAM` / `KILL_VACUOUS_MUTATION` (⇒ arm set re-derived once, before any PnL, as a registered amendment inheriting the consumed look) / `NOT_RUN_*` per blockers. R-ORIG is never selectable regardless of outcome; if R-ORIG out-performs beyond the band, the mechanism is falsified on that pair — the "edge" was the seam (draft §4.2/§9.4, retained).
