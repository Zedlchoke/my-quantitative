### Frozen available_at rule (G-PIT-V3) — calendar arithmetic (P-26)
Decision Friday F, 17:00 ET. Under the measured cadence (Wednesday release
through prior Tuesday):
- **Normal case**: latest safely-published observation = Tuesday of the
  decision week = **F − 3 calendar days** (a *later* T−1/T−2 rule would consume
  unpublished data — rejected).
- **Worst case (holiday-clustered weeks, computed)**: the scheduled Wednesday
  release itself falls on a federal holiday and slips a week → data through the
  *previous* week's Tuesday: **F − 10/11 calendar days** (dated examples:
  F=2018-07-06 with Wed 07-04 holiday → data through 06-26 = 10 days back;
  F=2025-12-26 with Wed 12-24/Thu 12-25 holidays → data through 12-16 = 10 days
  back; the measured Christmas-2025 instance had data through 12-15 = 11 days
  back for a 12-26 decision).

**FROZEN RULE: at a Friday 17:00 ET decision, consume the EIA spot series only
through the latest observation with date ≤ (F − 10 calendar days).**
Status: **EVIDENCED** against the current regime (stated release dates +
5 vintages + measured 9-day Christmas degradation); the *historical constancy*
of the weekly cadence before 2025-12 is **ASSERTED-conservative** (unmeasured —
if EIA posted faster in the past, this rule merely uses slightly staler data,
which cannot leak; it can only cost staleness, never look ahead).
