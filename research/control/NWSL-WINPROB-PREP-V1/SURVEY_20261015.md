## 2. Fixture ↔ results ↔ odds 1:1 join feasibility (measured facts, no assumptions)

- ESPN gives kickoff **UTC** + season id/type; nwslR gives naive **local**
  kickoff + stadium/city + scores + penalty fields; PM slugs encode
  `YYYY-MM-DD` + team pair. A deterministic 1:1 join
  (date + team-pair crosswalk, kickoff-UTC consistency bound like the WSL
  ≤3600 s law) is **plausible but UNPROVEN**: crosswalk coverage, team-name
  drift (e.g. FC Kansas City → Utah Royals → Kansas City Current; Western NY
  Flash → NC Courage; 2024 expansion + 2026 Boston Legacy FC / Denver
  Summit?) and same-day double-headers are lane tasks with measured join
  rates. Fail-closed: any ambiguous join = `CROSSWALK_MISS`, never guessed.
- Season/playoff separation (owner requirement): NWSL regular season ends in
  draws (no ET); playoffs have ET + penalties. Evidence paths: ESPN
  season-type field + nwslR `won_on_penalties`/penalty-score fields
  cross-checked; a playoff-row marker law must be **audited, not asserted**
  (nwslR season labels show irregular extra rows: 2023 137 vs 132 regular;
  2022 137; 2024 exactly 182 = the 14×26/2 regular-season count — marker law
  is an open audit item).
- 90-minute target law: regular-season rows = 90-min results natively;
  playoff rows need the ET/shootout flag (S2 penalty fields + S1 status
  text) before any 1X2 use — same discipline as the men's corpus
  (NOT_RUN_TARGET_NO_ET_FLAG pattern).
