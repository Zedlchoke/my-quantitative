**Calendar conventions (stated so the tiers are mechanically checkable):**

- (i) tzdata session/day-of-week/month geometry — free; does not affect tier.
- (ii) Market-holiday calendars (UK/US/JP): public deterministic, not staged. A
  row whose holiday logic is only a **skip/filter** keeps its tier with that
  exclusion marked "inactive until staged" (a declared default). A row whose
  core trigger arms on the holiday is capped at T2.
- (iii) Release/event **date** calendars (which day is MPC/CPI/NFP/ECB): public
  deterministic, not staged. A row whose trigger is price/vol-based but ARMS on
  the event is capped at T2 with "calendar archive staging" as the named
  prerequisite (dates only — trivially verifiable public record).
- (iv) **Value** archives (consensus numbers, surprise vintages, text, OI,
  auction tails, MOF amounts): real PIT data that does not exist in the repo →
  T3. Distinction from (iii) is deliberate: dates are deterministic; values
  carry sourcing/vintage discipline the program has not discharged.
- Couplings (the corpus's "Coupling:" lines) that require unevidenced data are
  **declared-off** rather than blocking; only unevidenced elements in the
  Signal/Trade lines park a row. This convention is disclosed because it is a
  judgment call; every declared-off coupling is named in the row's reason where
  material.
- The FX-spot-only vehicle at registration means energy legs can enter as
  **signal/gate inputs only** (WTI/Brent daily close are evidenced); any rule
  requiring an oil/gold/equity **execution leg** is T3 on vehicle grounds
  (e.g. USDCAD-S062/S081 two-leg pairs trades).
- **Pre-trade computability of exclusions (remediation F-10):** a filter that
  conditions on events known only EX-POST is not implementable as a live
  pre-trade rule even if a perfect historical archive existed. The binding
  case is intervention/event-date exclusions (MOF interventions are
  unscheduled and disclosed weeks later): **USDJPY-S020's corpus MOF
  exclusion must be DROPPED in its preregistered defaults**, and the row
  carries a **MECHANIZATION_DEVIATION** label in its triage reason (tier kept
  T2 — the price trigger is complete; the deviation from the corpus text is
  disclosed, not hidden). Distinguish from **price-level zone filters**
  (USDJPY-S086's intervention-zone check can be bound to a static price-band
  rule, computable pre-trade). This convention applies at the §5.2
  defaults-registration pass.

### 3.6 FORBIDDEN list (explicit, binding on the future engine)

1. **Future data of any kind** — any input whose value at decision time T
   depends on information first available after T (bar opens at/after T,
   later vintages, later revisions, later fills). Feature snapshots carry
   `available_time_utc`; a snapshot with `available_time_utc > T` aborts the
   decision fail-closed (BLOCKED_STALE), never substitutes.
2. **Current-fold realized PnL of any leg or of the portfolio** (fold-lock
   law, owner directive verbatim: looking at the losses of the fold you are
   about to confirm with is forbidden). The engine enforcement point: **every
   L2/L3/L5 feature is computed from data ≤ (fold start − 1-week embargo)
   only; the engine's feature-computation stage runs on a truncated panel
   (truncation-replay prefix-identical check, campaign §8.1), and any feature
   row referencing in-fold journal rows is a STOP-3 integrity trip.** The
   journal's re-derivation law (JOURNAL_SCHEMA §2.3: every blocked row's rule
   re-derivable from frozen policy inputs at `ts_decision_utc`) is the
   audit-side enforcement.
3. **Regime-prediction variables claiming forward-looking information** —
   features whose justification is a forecast of future regime (e.g.
   "VIX will spike", "trend will continue") rather than a measured backward
   state; in particular any fitted classifier/regressor whose target variable
   is future returns of the current fold. Measured state (VIX level, RV
   percentile, Hurst estimate) is lawful; a model trained on the fold being
   traded is not (promotion law (a)-(d) is the test).
4. **Any feature whose value depends on test-fold outcomes** — including
   features tuned (even implicitly, by threshold choice) on test-fold
   behavior; all thresholds frozen at Stage-B freeze, from TRAIN only.
5. **Unevidenced inputs** (the §1 T3 list): consensus/surprise vintages, CB
   texts, yields/swaps (incl. SOFR and SONIA — only EFFR/VIX/Brent−WTI are
   evidenced), equities, non-evidenced commodities, option quotes, OI,
   positioning, retail sentiment — none of these may enter the policy until
   their PIT dossier passes.
6. **In-fold loss-taxonomy feedback** — the LOSS_* primary-cause tags of the
   current fold may not select gating rules for that fold (JOURNAL_SCHEMA §3
   promotion law, verbatim).

## 4. MISSED-PROFIT + SWITCHING-COST MEASUREMENT PLAN

The owner's question (verbatim intent): can a policy using only pre-trade
information time each leg ON/OFF such that, AFTER counting missed profitable
trades and ALL state-switching costs, the portfolio beats its baselines?
Therefore every candidate gating-rule evaluation records BOTH sides of the
ledger — losses avoided AND profits missed — plus the full switching bill.
Cross-references: `journal/JOURNAL_SCHEMA.md` (row schema §1, reconciliation
§2, loss taxonomy §3, co-loss reporting §4) and `journal/FILL_ACCOUNTING_SPEC.md`
(spread-once law §2, cost guard §8, stress tiers §5) — both files EXIST in
`journal/`; the cross-references below are live.

### 4.1 Shadow always-on book (the counterfactual machine)

Per leg, per fold, the engine maintains the **B1 shadow**: the same leg run
always-eligible under the same kernel/tier/calendar (PASS_BARS §1 B1). Every
policy-blocked or policy-skipped entry is matched to its B1 counterfactual
twin (same decision timestamp, same rule, same size law). The shadow book is
a measurement device on TRAIN folds only during Stage-A/B; in Stage-C the
same machinery IS the B1 baseline arm. Counterfactual twins are journaled as
new rows (`counterfactual=true`), never edits of policy rows (append-only
law).

### 4.2 Journal fields (additions to journal_row@1; all other fields per JOURNAL_SCHEMA §1)

Decision-time block additions:
- `eligibility_rule_id` — which frozen L2 rule produced ON/PROBATION/OFF
  (already implied by `eligibility_state`; now an explicit id into the frozen
  rule table).
- `onoff_features` — the §3 feature vector digest actually consumed
  (`{feature_id: value_digest}`), making every state decision re-derivable
  (extends `feature_pit_snapshot`).

Counterfactual twin block (on shadow rows):
- `twin_of_order_id`, `would_fill_price`, `would_fill_side_quote`,
  `would_spread_once`, `would_slip`, `would_commission`, `would_swap`,
  `would_net_pnl_usd`, `would_exit_reason_code`, `would_hold_bars` — the
  always-on twin's fill and outcome under the identical kernel.

Fold-rollup block (per leg per fold, in the ONOFF_LEDGER — a derived
append-only table, schema `onoff_ledger@1`, one row per (leg, fold, rule_id)):
- `entries_taken`, `entries_blocked`, `entries_deferred` — counts from
  journal `outcome` enums (FILLED vs BLOCKED_*/DEFERRED_COST_GUARD/
  KILLSWITCH_FLAT/NETTED_TO_ZERO)
- `losses_avoided_usd` = Σ |`would_net_pnl_usd`| over blocked/deferred twins
  with `would_net_pnl_usd < 0`
- `profits_missed_usd` = Σ `would_net_pnl_usd` over blocked/deferred twins
  with `would_net_pnl_usd > 0`
- `state_switch_count` = number of eligibility state changes (ON↔OFF,
  ON↔PROBATION) in the fold, and the number of position changes they induced
- `switch_cost_usd` = Σ over policy-induced state changes of
  `|Δ| × (half_spread_cell + U3_band)` charged at **the measured spread cell
  of the actual fill hour** (C1 per-hour p50 cell; the p90 ladder re-pricing
  is PASS_BARS P6d) plus the U3 band of that hour (0.2 pip non-event /
  1.0 pip event window), commission per FILL_ACCOUNTING §2 — spread charged
  EXACTLY ONCE through the fill price, per the spread-once law; no second
  crossing line
- `net_switching_bill_vs_always_on_usd` = Σ policy state-change costs − Σ
  B1 twin costs over the matched entries (the EXTRA cost of toggling vs
  letting the leg run)
- `onoff_net_contribution_usd` = `losses_avoided_usd − profits_missed_usd −
  switch_cost_usd` (see 4.3)
- `policy_net_pnl_usd`, `b1_net_pnl_usd`, `delta_vs_b1_usd` — the
  unambiguous bottom line per leg per fold.

### 4.3 Headline metric

```
ONOFF_NET_CONTRIBUTION(leg, fold) = losses_avoided − profits_missed − switching_costs
```

computed per fold, **reported with its sign** (negative = the gating rule
cost more than it saved on that fold), plus the pooled TEST-window sum at
Stage-C. Companion quantities reported alongside (never omitted): the same
metric at ×1.5 cost tier (P6a semantics) and at the p90 switching ladder
(P6d); the per-fold sign pattern (how many folds positive); and
`delta_vs_b1` which nets everything (PnL + costs) and is the quantity P1
consumes at portfolio level. Rationale: `ONOFF_NET_CONTRIBUTION` isolates the
TIMING decision (what did gating itself add/subtract), while `delta_vs_b1`
includes sizing/netting effects; reporting both prevents a policy from
claiming timing credit for netting savings. No cherry side: the avoidable-loss
ledger law (JOURNAL_SCHEMA §3: both skipped losses AND skipped profits
mandatory) is inherited verbatim.

### 4.4 Costing details (binding)

- Switch costs price at the ACTUAL fill hour's measured C1 cell (per-hour
  p50/p90 schedule, panel venue) + U3 band of that hour; the p90 ladder
  (every state change at p90) is a mandatory re-run (P6d), not an option.
- Sunday-reopen deferrals charge C3 (measured); rollover-window fills charge
  C2 per the corrected Mon-Thu cells flagged by Lane D (program-wide
  correction pending — noted as an open dependency; a Gate/leg design that
  avoids 21:00-23:00 UTC fills, as VOLPROF does, is immune).
- Swap accrues on any policy state change that creates an overnight position
  (Wednesday ×3); intraday-flat legs keep the structural-zero-swap property
  only if BOTH the leg rule and its gating rule preserve the 19:55-UTC flat.
- Blocked/deferred rows carry NO fill/cost block (FILL_ACCOUNTING §8) —
  their cost lives only in the counterfactual twin fields.

## 5. SEQUENCING RECOMMENDATION (Stage-A compute bounds ~900-1,200 engine runs)

### 5.1 Arithmetic

Runnable set: 75 T1 + 75 T2 = 150 corpus rows + 9 VOLPROF class candidates
(= 159 admission candidates). Priced conservatively: Stage-A diagnostic ≈ 1
engine run per candidate; VOLPROF controls are re-evaluations of the same arm
(4 controls × 9 arms = 36 control runs, zero extra scored cells); ×1.5/×2.0
cost stress ≈ 2 more runs per surviving candidate. Worst case ≈ 159 + 36 +
~300 ≈ 495 runs — inside the 900-1,200 bound with headroom for re-runs,
truncation-replay verification (§8.1) and Stage-B policy variants. The bound
does NOT force a tier cut; it forces an ORDER.

### 5.2 Recommended order (breadth first, cost second)

1. **Wave 1 — exact-arm dry-runs, ALL 75 T1 rows + 9 VOLPROF candidates (84
   runs, no PnL printed per RGC law, campaign §8.5):** identities, decision
   counts vs the 20/3/100 floors, fill counts, cost-bill totals, staleness/
   UNAVAILABLE counts. T1 first because parameter preregistration is not
   needed — zero risk of defaults drifting; and the wave immediately prices
   the feasibility attrition the campaign expects. Include ALL FOUR SLATES'
   T1 legs (G1 8, G2 9, G3 3+9, G4 6 — overlapping sets) so slate-level
   feasibility is known after one wave.
2. **Wave 2 — the T2 defaults-registration pass (document, not compute):**
   for each of the 75 T2 rows, preregister the declared defaults named in the
   triage reason (one frozen value each, no grids — VOLPROF precedent), plus
   the calendar-archive staging (class-iii) for the rows that need it and the
   G-PIT-V3 daily-clock staleness adaptation for oil consumers. The pass
   applies the §1.1 pre-trade-computability test: filters conditioning on
   ex-post-known events are DROPPED (USDJPY-S020's MOF exclusion — the one
   MECHANIZATION_DEVIATION row; its defaults and the deviation are frozen in
   the arm identity), and zone filters are bound to price-level rules
   (USDJPY-S086). Then dry-run the T2 legs of the four slates first (G1 4,
   G2 3, G4 4), then remaining T2 rows by family breadth.
3. **Wave 3 — Stage-A TRAIN diagnostics (the first PnL-bearing runs, TRAIN
   folds only, outcome-labeled):** VOLPROF class battery (9 arms + 36 control
   re-evaluations, the most information-dense block: fresh observable,
   decisive controls), then slate-priority order G2 → G1 → G4 → G3-anchors.
   G2 (session intraday) first among corpus slates because (a) decision
   counts are deepest — floors resolve fastest; (b) it carries the program's
   largest un-measured correlation risk (session-class, VOLPROF §11); (c) M1
   replay cost is front-loaded once and shared with G3's class runs.
4. **T3 stays parked at zero runs.** Its 144 rows convert only on named owner
   actions (rates/swaps dossier incl. SOFR/SONIA → up to 11 candidate rows
   plus the 3 departed; consensus vintages → up to 48;
   gold/equities/commodities/OI/news-tag dossiers → the rest). The triage
   TSV is the unblock map.
5. **Stage-B (after Stage-A):** complementarity measured on TRAIN (JOURNAL
   §4 outputs), slate selection, caps, conflict rules, switching-cost budget
   (§4 fields), then the frozen policy spec + freeze SHA. Stage-C scored runs
   are GO-gated and NOT sized here.

### 5.3 What the first fold of diagnostics must report (Wave 1 deliverable, plus first Stage-A fold)

Per candidate: compiled y/n + param digest; decision counts (full window /
per fold / aggregate) vs floors 20/3/100 with the typed NOT_RUN_THIN where

slip / commission / swap) at ×1.0 and ×1.5; staleness and UNAVAILABLE counts;
GROSS-vs-NET gap (COST_DOMINATED candidates typed); MECHANIZATION_DEVIATION
