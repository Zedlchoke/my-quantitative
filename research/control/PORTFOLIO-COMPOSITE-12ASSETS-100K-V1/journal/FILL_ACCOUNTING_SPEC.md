## 1. Price conventions

1. **Mid.** `mid(bar) = (bid + ask) / 2` computed from the SAME M1 bar's
   two-sided quotes: `mid_open = (bid_open + ask_open)/2`,
   `mid_close = (bid_close + ask_close)/2`. Bid and ask must come from the
   same bar and same side (open with open, close with close); mixing sides is
   an integrity trip.
2. **Decision uses completed bars only.** A decision at timestamp T may read
   exclusively bars fully closed before T (the last completed M1 bar is the
   one spanning [T−1min, T)). Bar-open values at or after T are invisible to
   the decision (PIT law; feature snapshots carry `available_time_utc`).
3. **Fill at NEXT bar open.** The order generated by a decision at T fills at
   the OPEN of the next bar (the bar spanning [T, T+1min) for M1 clocks; the
   [20:00, 20:01) bar for the weekly Friday-20:00 clock — campaign §1):
   - **buys:** `fill_price = ask_open + slip`
   - **sells:** `fill_price = bid_open − slip`
   with `slip` = U3 band per side: **0.2 pip (0.00002) non-event / 1.0 pip
   (0.0001) inside event windows** (rollover 21:00–23:00 UTC per UNIFIED §7;
   CB blackout ± window; top-tier data releases ±30 min; Sunday reopen first
   15 min — declared pessimistic).
4. `mid_at_fill` = the mid_open of the SAME fill bar. The fill's crossing
   distance is `|fill_price − mid_at_fill| = half_spread_at_fill + slip`
   (buys: `ask_open − mid_open + slip`; sells: `mid_open − bid_open + slip`)
   — §2 turns this into the spread-once law.
5. **Worst-fill grading on exception fills** (stops/gaps/reopen through
   levels), inherited from COST_ACCOUNTING_SPEC §2.6 and made explicit here
   (skeptic F-16 remediation): a graded exception fill executes at the WORST
   of the fill bar's open and close on the quoted side — buys:
   `max(ask_open, ask_close)`, sells: `min(bid_open, bid_close)` — then ± the
   U3 slip band, NEVER at the stop or level price (a bar trading through a
   level fills at the graded market price, not the level). `mid_at_fill`
   stays the fill bar's OPEN mid (§1.4), so the distance of the graded fill
   beyond the ordinary `quoted_side_open ± slip_band` fill is booked as GAP
   EXCESS inside the slippage-beyond-halfspread line (§6); the spread-once
   identity `spread_once + slip_beyond_halfspread == |fill − mid_at_fill|`
   still holds per fill, and the row flags `LOSS_FILL` candidacy. Fixture
   `fx6_worst_fill_grading` pins this path with hand-checked numbers.

## 2. Spread-once law (the anti-double-charge law)

**The spread cost of a fill IS its distance from mid:**

```
cross_cost(fill) = fill_price − mid_at_fill     (buys,  ≥ 0)
cross_cost(fill) = mid_at_fill − fill_price     (sells, ≥ 0)
```

By §1.3–1.4 construction `cross_cost = half_spread_at_fill + slip_applied`.
This quantity is charged to the book **EXACTLY ONCE — through the fill
price itself** (cash moves at `fill_price`; marks move at `mid`). The
journal's cost block for that fill MUST NOT contain any ADDITIONAL "spread"
line: the only crossing entries permitted are the two attribution sub-lines
whose sum equals `cross_cost` exactly:

```
spread_once(fill)            = |quoted_side − mid_at_fill|   # half of the quoted spread at the fill bar
slip_beyond_halfspread(fill) = |fill_price − quoted_side|    # the U3 band applied (band + any §1.5 excess)
spread_once + slip_beyond_halfspread == cross_cost           # exact identity, per fill
```

(`quoted_side` = `ask_open` for buys, `bid_open` for sells; these map to the
journal fields `spread_pips_charged` (C1/C2/C3 cell id) and
`slippage_beyond_spread` (U3 band id).) A cost block whose crossing lines sum
to MORE than `cross_cost` is a double charge; LESS is an under-charge. Either
mismatch is a STOP-3 integrity trip. There is no third crossing line — no
"half-spread surcharge", no "spread p50/p90 schedule fee on top of the fill".
The measured spread cells enter only as (a) validation bands for the
bar-quoted spread, (b) the pricing source for window fills (§3, dual-sourced
per cell — the corrected Lane D Mon–Thu roll cells govern over the table's
hour-shifted column), and (c) the cost-guard pre-commit estimate and the p90
switching-ladder re-run (PASS_BARS_V2 P6d).

**Commission is separate, per side:** U2 = 0.35 bps of traded notional:
`commission = 0.000035 × |Δ| × fill_price` (units × fill price = USD notional
for USD-quoted pairs). Charged on BOTH entry and exit legs of every state
change; never amortized (COST_ACCOUNTING_SPEC §2.9).

**Swap U4/U5 accrues per night held:**
`swap_usd(night) = |u| × pip_size × 1.0 pip × multiplier`, charged AGAINST
the position in BOTH directions (never income — pessimistic symmetry), booked
`cash −= swap_usd` at each accrual event. Accrual boundary 22:00 UTC.
Frozen swap calendar — the **DECLARED U4/U5 realization, a frozen modeling
calendar, NOT a venue-measured fact** (skeptic F-15 softening: no Interest
Accruals statement exists anywhere in the workspace; the owner statement
replaces this calendar whole, and any fixture number computed under it is a
`NET_DEFAULT_COST`-band number). Under this declaration a continuously held
position accrues exactly 7 pip-nights per 7-calendar-night week
(value-date-consistent: the weekend is carried by Wednesday's ×3):

| accrual event (22:00 UTC) | multiplier |
|---|---|
| Sunday (the reopen boundary) | ×1 |
| Monday | ×1 |
| Tuesday | ×1 |
| Wednesday | ×3 (spot T+2 rolls settlement Fri→Mon: the weekend) |
| Thursday | ×1 |
| Friday (the close boundary) | ×0 — no accrual event exists at market close |
| Saturday | no event |

**Accrual membership rule:** an accrual event at E charges iff `u(E⁻) ≠ 0`,
where `u(E⁻)` is the position from all fills with timestamp strictly before
E. A fill timestamped exactly at E belongs to the [E, E+1min) bar and neither
sees nor triggers E's accrual; a position closed exactly at E was open at E⁻
and DOES accrue (held through the boundary — pessimistic reading).

## 4. FX-spot book identity (quote-currency accounting, $100k book)

**State** (per symbol; USD-quote pairs at registration):

```
u_t        : signed position in base-currency units (EUR for EURUSD)
cash_usd_t : USD cash
equity_t   = cash_usd_t + u_t × mid_close_t      (mark at mid_close each bar)
```

**Per-event deltas (the only ways state changes):**

```
buy Δ units at fill f:   cash −= Δ×f + commission ;  u += Δ
sell Δ units at fill f:  cash += Δ×f − commission ;  u −= Δ
nightly accrual event E: cash −= swap_usd(E)          (u unchanged)
mark (every bar):        equity = cash + u × mid_close(bar)
```

Opening cash $100,000.00; `u_0 = 0`. Cash MAY go negative inside the margin
fence (FX 30:1, U12) — that is leverage, not an error; the margin fence gates
new risk only, never marks (COST_ACCOUNTING_SPEC §2.5). One net position per
symbol (netting OMS precedent); virtual sub-position attribution per §7.

**UNAVAILABLE ≠ 0.** A missing bid/ask for a mark or a fill aborts the
affected decision fail-closed (never a 0-price fill, never a $0 mark): the
leg's order is not placed / the order cannot be priced → it dies or defers
per §3/§8 and the equity cell is reported UNAVAILABLE (no forward/backward
fill). Fill-bar search: if the next bar at T is missing, search forward
bar-by-bar up to the next decision timestamp; the first available bar prices
the fill (window cells apply if the deferral crossed into §3 windows); none
found → order cancelled, journaled blocked (`UNAVAILABLE_FILL_BAR`), no cash
effect.

## 5. Stress tiers (×1.0 / ×1.5 / ×2.0)

At tier τ every VARIABLE component scales: the crossing distance and every
rate — `fill = mid_at_fill ± τ·(half_spread_at_fill + slip_band)`,
`commission = τ·0.000035·|Δ|·fill`, `swap = τ·1.0 pip/night` (Wed ×3
preserved), C2/C3 cells' p90 × τ. The spread-once law and the §6 invariant
hold at EVERY tier by construction (the crossing lines still sum to
`|fill − mid|`); the fixtures encode τ = 1.0 and the runner may sweep τ with
proportionally scaled expectations. Non-variable fences (margin, caps) are
not scaled (PASS_BARS_V2 §6).

## 6. The equity-decomposition invariant (the accounting gate)

For any run segment (fixture, fold, or whole campaign), over ALL fills,
commissions, swap accruals and open positions marked at the final mid:

```
Σ(trade gross vs mid) − Σ(spread_once) − Σ(commission) − Σ(swap)
                    − Σ(slip_beyond_halfspread)  =  equity_T − equity_0      [to 1e-9]
```

with

```
trade gross vs mid  =  mid_valued(exit) − mid_valued(entry)   per unit, per trade
   closed trade:     units × ( mid_at_exit_fill − mid_at_entry_fill )
   open position:    u × ( mid_close(final mark) − mid_at_entry_fill )   [unrealized leg]
mid_valued(x)        =  mid_at_fill of the trade's own fill bar
```

**So that spread appears ONLY inside fill-vs-mid:** the gross term is
mid-to-mid (it contains no spread); the entire cost of crossing lives in the
two crossing lines, which per fill sum exactly to `|fill − mid|`; nothing
else touches the fill price. **Proof (round trip, one unit, no swap):**
`equity_T − equity_0 = (f_sell − f_buy) − comm_in − comm_out` from the §4
deltas; substitute `f_buy = mid_in + h_in + s_in`, `f_sell = mid_out − h_out
− s_out` (h = half-spread, s = slip): `= (mid_out − mid_in) − (h_in + h_out)
− (s_in + s_out) − comm_in − comm_out` = gross-vs-mid − Σspread_once −
Σslip − Σcommission. Adding swap accruals (pure cash debits) and open
positions (marked at mid) extends the identity term-by-term. The fixtures
verify this to 1e-9 in float (residuals observed 0 in Decimal, ≤1e-12 in
float).

A scored run whose journal fails this reconciliation (per bar AND per run)
is VOID: `NOT_RUN_INTEGRITY`, STOP-3, remediation loop — no silent continue
(JOURNAL_SCHEMA §2).

## 7. Netting execution model (two legs, one asset)

1. Eligibility (L2) runs BEFORE netting (L4): a blocked/ineligible leg casts
   NO vote; its row records the would-be vote and the exclusion (§8).
2. All eligible legs on one symbol are risk-weight VOTERS:
   `net_target = truncate_{±1}( Σ_i sign_i × risk_weight_i )`. Partial
   agreement ⇒ smaller net size; a tie at zero (nonzero votes, net = 0) ⇒
   asset FLAT that decision, both votes journaled `NETTED_TO_ZERO` (no fill,
   no cash effect — §8 law).
3. **The BOOK trades only the NET.** One net order per (symbol, decision):
   `Δnet = net_target_units − u`. Fills, costs, cash and margin are computed
   on the net book position (COST_ACCOUNTING_SPEC §2.1); virtual
   sub-positions are attribution only.
4. **Journal law for netted execution:** per leg, a VOTE row (decision-time
   block + `direction_vote` + `desired_risk_units` + netting block:
   `{net_target_units, leg_contribution, offset_by[]}`, outcome `FILLED` via
   the net order, fill fields NULL — the leg owns no cash effect), PLUS one
   NET-ORDER row per executed net order (order_kind OPEN/CLOSE/ADJUST,
   carrying the actual Δ, fill_price, both crossing lines, commission, and
   the cash/position deltas). Cash changes appear exactly once — on net-order
   rows.
5. **Virtual attribution (reporting only, never re-charged):** per leg,
   `virtual gross vs mid = virtual_units × (mid_exit − mid_entry)`; crossing
   and commission costs of each net fill are attributed pro-rata to
   `|virtual_units_i| / Σ_j |virtual_units_j|`. Attributions sum to the book
   totals exactly (fixture fx3 pins this).

## 8. Blocked, deferred and netted-to-zero signals

**No cash/position effect. Ever.** A row with outcome ∈ {`BLOCKED_STALE`,
`BLOCKED_FLOOR`, `BLOCKED_COST_BUDGET`, `BLOCKED_RISK_CAP`, `NETTED_TO_ZERO`,
`DEFERRED_COST_GUARD`, `KILLSWITCH_FLAT`} carries:

- the decision-time block ONLY (ts_decision_utc, fold/cycle/sleeve ids,
  symbol, family_id, mech_key, `feature_pit_snapshot`, `direction_vote`,
  `desired_risk_units`, `entry_reason_code`, `eligibility_state`,
  `risk_rule_state`, `portfolio_state`) — the decision-state snapshot;
- `outcome` + `blocked_reason_detail` (frozen enums preferred);
- for `DEFERRED_COST_GUARD`: order_kind `DEFER` + the guard block
  (`{window, cap_usd, trailing_spend_usd}`) + the pre-commit estimate
  (`{est_spread_once, est_slip, est_commission, est_total}` computed from the
  decision bar's quotes on the proposed Δ);
- NO fill block, NO cost block, NO cash/position delta. Fill fields are NULL
  (absent), never zero.

**Re-derivation law** (JOURNAL_SCHEMA §2.3): every such row's stated rule
must be re-derivable from the frozen policy inputs at `ts_decision_utc`
(1% sample, full recompute on STOP-3 trips). **Deferral semantics:** a
deferred change defers the EXECUTION, not the decision — at the next decision
the target is recomputed from scratch; if it still differs from the current
position and the guard allows, it executes then (fixture fx5). The deferred
intent is never a standing order.

**Cost guard:** before committing any size change, estimate its all-in cost
(`est_spread_once + est_slip + est_commission` on the proposed Δ at the
decision bar's quotes); if the trailing state-change cost budget is exhausted
(`trailing_spend ≥ cap`), defer NON-URGENT changes (risk-increasing or
neutral); risk-REDUCING closes are always allowed (R1). Guard parameters
(window, cap) are frozen at Stage-B from the TRAIN switching ladder.

## 9. GROSS / NET dual books

Identical fills and positions; the GROSS book applies zero costs (drag
attribution and HYPOTHETICAL-labeled comparability only); the NET book
applies this spec in full and is the ONLY decision-grade book
(COST_ACCOUNTING_SPEC §3.1). Every NET number under declared bands carries
`NET_DEFAULT_COST`. PASS bars consume NET only.
