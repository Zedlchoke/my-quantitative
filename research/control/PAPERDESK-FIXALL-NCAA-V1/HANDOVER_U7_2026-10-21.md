## 2. The frozen law (exact content, mirrored by the module constants)

- **Sharp-price gate (§2):** take the PM price only when it beats the
  Pinnacle NO-VIG reference at decision time by the frozen margin,
  PM-fee-adjusted: `p_pm_eff = p_pm + 0.05·p_pm·(1−p_pm)` (taker fee ONCE
  — the FEE_ONCE law); gate `p_pm_eff ≤ p_pin_leg·(1−0.03)`.
  **SHARP_PRICE_MARGIN = 3%, stated explicitly in the spec.**
- **Blended probability law (§3):** EV from a frozen log-odds blend of the
  MODEL probability and the PINNACLE no-vig probability — NEVER the PM
  price being taken. The blend weight is **recorded as a FITTING PROTOCOL,
  not a fitted number** (rolling-origin grid on pre-era data, log-score
  objective, w locked by registered amendment BEFORE the first new-law
  decision). The shadow uses the PROVISIONAL identity weight 0.5, labeled
  as such, and journals `p_model`/`p_pin_leg` so any locked w re-applies
  offline.
- **Disagreement-scaled edge threshold (§4):**
  `threshold = 0.05 + 0.5·|p_model − p_pin_leg|`; TAKE ⇔ sharp gate PASS
  AND `EV_blend ≥ threshold`.
- **Kill gate (§4):** real cost accounting (fee once + 0.5%-per-side
  slippage), minimum **n = 200 scored decisions**, verdict on an
  **untouched holdout** (data at/after era-open only), parameter
  sensitivity **±10–20% must not flip the verdict**; kill line: realized
  CLV_pin mean < 0 or ROI ≤ 0 over the first 200 scored decisions.
- **Per-league auto-cutoff (§5), keyed on the U6 CLV plane:** a league
  pauses ITSELF when its rolling CLV_pin (mean over the last ≥ 30 scored
  bets) drops below **−5%**; evidence = sidecar record ids; lift is
  owner-only like the QD1 law.
- **Scoring only on future data (§6):** no new-law number may ever be
  computed on data at or before the era-open date; the §3 fit is the ONLY
  thing allowed to look backward.
