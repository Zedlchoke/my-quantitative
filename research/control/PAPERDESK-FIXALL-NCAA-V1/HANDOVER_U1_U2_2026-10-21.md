`ai_scorecard@2` (signature kept: `ledger_rows`, `opinions_dir`; new
optional `results_by_mid=None`, `gate_events_path=None`):

1. **Results plane law**: veto ACTUAL results = the ledger's SETTLEMENT row
   for the match when present; else the caller's `results_by_mid`

   `veto_scored_actual` (vetoes with a real final result); same
   suggest-only law; the proposal now discloses its denominator and the
   excluded unscored count.
3. **Honest classes**: `not_placed_no_verdict` decisions counted with
   hypothetical PnL when the frozen gate-block evidence shape
   (hypothetical_leg/stake/odds) is present AND a result is obtainable

   `len(opinions_dir/.invalid/*.json)`; `gate_events` aggregated from
   `gate_events.jsonl` when `gate_events_path` is passed — kind counts +
   `verdicts_by_model` from OPINION_PRODUCED events.
4. **Breakdowns**: `per_league` (decision rows), `per_model` (opinion
   model_id), `per_reason_code` (counts + INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE share).
   Hypothetical money still uses THE FROZEN SETTLE LAW (win +stake·(d−1),
   lose −stake, VOID family 0).
