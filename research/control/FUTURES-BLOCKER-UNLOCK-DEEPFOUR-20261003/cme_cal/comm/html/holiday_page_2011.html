CME Group Holiday Calendar
 

CME Group observes U.S. recognized holidays. On CME Group recognized holidays, open outcry trading and CME Globex tranding observe different opening and closing times, depending on markets traded. Opening and closing times are also affected on the business days prior to or after a holiday in both open outcry and CME Globex Trading.
