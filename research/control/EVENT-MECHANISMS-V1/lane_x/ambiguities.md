# lane_x AMBIGUITY LOG (P2 protocol)

Where G3_FREEZE.md / addenda are silent or underdetermined, lane_x chose its
own reading BEFORE any comparison with the other implementation.  Each entry:
the ambiguity, the law that leaves it open, lane_x's choice.  These are
recorded so every G5 mismatch can be traced to a reading difference
(SPEC_AMBIGUITY) or a genuine violation (DEFECT).

| ID | Ambiguity | lane_x reading |
|----|-----------|----------------|
