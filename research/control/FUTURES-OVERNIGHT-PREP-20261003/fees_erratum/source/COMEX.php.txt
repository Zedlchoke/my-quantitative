Additionally, it is the client's obligation to notify IBKR of any incentive program membership. IBKR will need to independently verify the account's status with the exchange. If verified, reduced fees will go into effect within 2 weeks of IBKR receiving verification. Reduced fees will not be retroactively applied for trading done prior to IBKR being notified and confirming the account's incentive program status.
