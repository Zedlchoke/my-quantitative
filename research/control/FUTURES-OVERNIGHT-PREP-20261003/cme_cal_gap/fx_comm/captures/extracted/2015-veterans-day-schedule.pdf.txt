 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
In recognition of the Veterans Day holiday on Wednesday, November 11, 2015, CME Group trading floors 
will be closed for Interest Rate and FX products. Regular CME Globex trading hours will be in effect. Given 
the bank holiday and trading floor closures on these days, special settlement procedures will be used for FX 
and Interest Rate products that have trade dates of November 11, 2015. 
 
Veterans Day Settlement Procedures: 
 
• Daily settlement prices for Interest Rate and FX futures and options will be determined at 12:00 
p.m. CT on Veterans Day  
• Settlement procedures will be modified for the holiday

Additional Notes 
• All settlement banks will be closed on Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2015. 
Accordingly, there will be no movement of margin or settlement funds on that date. 
 
• There will be no intra-day banking and settlement cycle on November 11, 2015. 
Consequently, all trade activity for trades on November 11 will be marked-to-market based 
on the settlement prices as determined per the above-described methodology (see table). 
 
• For Veterans Day, the next movement of funds associated with the margin and settlement 
obligations from end-of-day Tuesday, November 10, and the full trade date of November 11, 
will occur on Thursday morning, November 12.
