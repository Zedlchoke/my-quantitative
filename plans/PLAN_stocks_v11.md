1. **filing-event reaction** (mandatory lane): event-time books around

   shape, amendment depth as conditioners (restatement-strain and

   through P2 coverage + the standard novelty gates).

### 2.1 Book-form provision (preregistered, new in V11)

A proposal MAY construct its book on sector-residual returns
(SPY/sector-hedged legs) where its mechanism justifies it; the
justification is frozen inside the proposal before selection. This
provision changes permissible construction ONLY: the frozen exposure,
ETF-share and SPY-excess gates apply unchanged to whatever book form is
chosen, and the D2 novelty rho is measured on conditioning exposures so
that a hedged relabelling of a rejected ancestor still dies. Rationale on
record: sector-beta drowning has killed or confounded the program's stocks
families since V2; better book engineering for NEW mechanisms is not
