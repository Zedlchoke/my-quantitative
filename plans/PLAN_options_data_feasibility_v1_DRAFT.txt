1. Findings and recommendation
Start with a bounded Deribit ETH/BTC options data-feasibility prototype because
production public REST returned option instruments, an option book, and history
in this session. This prioritizes access, not profitability. Keep US index/ETF
options as the second research branch. FX options remain NOT_RUN_PROVIDER_ABSENT;
spot-FX candle/pricing APIs do not establish an FX-options chain.

The direct probe returned 816 ETH option instruments, 169 hourly bars for a
sample option and 721 hourly ETH-PERPETUAL bars. An endpoint can include the
current unfinished bar. Bars are not 169/721 independent samples, do not prove
continuous trading, and must pass timestamp/completeness checks before use.
A bounded production WebSocket probe also received 12 ticker updates spanning
the sample option and ETH-PERPETUAL. This establishes a short real-time sample,
not sustained uptime, gap recovery, persistent liquidity or order execution. The sample contract minimum is 1 ETH option; its
premium being cheap is not evidence that shorting it fits $1,000 risk limits.
Re-run the saved read-only probe to refresh these time-specific counts.

2. Candidate families (hypotheses, not recommendations to trade)
A. Equity-index volatility-risk premium: fully collateralized put-writing is a
published benchmark/control; research defined-risk verticals as a separate
hypothesis. A bought wing changes returns and costs; the put-write benchmark
does not prove a spread strategy profitable. Index beta, crash losses and cash
interest must be attributed separately. XSP is cash-settled European-style;
actual quoted spread width, fees and minimum contract may still fail $1,000.
B. Crypto options relative volatility: compare implied variance with a frozen,
lagged realized-variance forecast; evaluate a capped-risk vertical or long-premium
structure only after executable chain data, unit accounting, collateral and
liquidity pass. IV greater than past realized volatility alone is not an edge.
No naked short, martingale, post-loss size escalation or unlimited exposure.
C. Term structure / calendar: secondary candidate, deferred. Different expiries,
vega/skew, exit liquidity, early-leg expiry and collateral make loss accounting
more complex. Do not call calendars automatically defined-risk.
Do not start from single-stock earnings bets, 0DTE or continuously delta-hedged
straddles: their event, gamma and execution burdens need stronger data/cost
support. These are scope choices for the first study, not claims they cannot work.

3. Capital and risk contract
Paper equity is exactly USD 1,000, with no simulated deposit/reset to hide losses.
Max portfolio drawdown, per-position worst-case loss, concurrent positions,
daily loss, fee/API budget and holding period are UNDECIDED. New entries blocked
until the contract is frozen. A 1% per-trade risk example means $10, but 1% is
an illustration and has not been approved as a limit.
For a same-expiry USD-settled put credit vertical, theoretical expiry loss is
(strike width * multiplier * quantity) - net credit + costs. Quantity is an
integer multiple of venue min_trade_amount. No fractional-contract fiction.
For inverse coin-settled instruments, prove native currency cashflows, USD
conversion, collateral price exposure, margin and settlement transitions first;
do not reuse a USD spread formula as the whole account risk model. Position
liquidation and legging can invalidate a simple expiry payoff bound.
Paper slippage/fees and ongoing data/AI operating costs must be disclosed in
USD and relative to the $1,000 account. Missing fees are BLOCKED, not zero.

4. Data acceptance before Python strategy evaluation
- instrument identity, multiplier, tick/lot, exercise, expiry and settlement;
- exchange timestamp AND local receipt time; lag, clock skew and sequence gaps;
- bid/ask and size for both legs; depth at actual quantity, spread and staleness;
- immutable raw responses, endpoint/query, hashes and versioned normalized rows;
- actual history availability, expired-contract census and survivorship checks;
- keep trade candles, indicative quotes, mark prices and executable books distinct;
- discard unfinished candles from indicators, log gaps and no-trade intervals;
- source terms/permissions and operating budget reviewed before continuous capture.
Underlying bars may test code or forecast features. They cannot establish option
fills, realized option P&L or historical volatility-surface dynamics. Begin
prospective option-book capture if bid/ask history is unavailable. A few days of
capture is a plumbing check, not sustainable strategy evidence.
